A comforting native dining hall right beside Petron and AUP.

Founded by Evelyn "Tia Belle" Wise, the restaurant was built to give Manila travelers and Southern locals an authentic, unhurried Filipino dining experience along Santa Rosa–Tagaytay Road.

Step inside to the smell of freshly sautéed garlic, rich annatto shrimp broth, and freshly steamed native kakanin. Whether you're stopping by for a warm merienda of Pichi-Pichi or picking up bilaos for a family celebration, everything is cooked in generous home-style portions.
